The story kits

One kit per tale: an illustrated card, an object to keep, and a small ritual to share once the book is closed.

What is inside

Each kit fits in a small linen pouch. Inside: an illustrated card that tells the ritual and gives the gestures, and the object that goes with it — hazelnuts, an acorn to plant, a hand-painted wooden disc. Nothing electronic, nothing to install.

The wooden discs are painted one by one. The acorns and hazelnuts are gathered in the forest of Zoufftgen, the one in the tales.

The cards are printed in French, like the books themselves.

A word about shipping

A kit ordered on its own travels in its own parcel, and the post charges a small parcel almost like a large one. Added to a book order it changes almost nothing: the parcel stays in the same weight bracket.

And if you happen to pass through Zoufftgen, collection by appointment is always possible: write to me.
